## Four district designs

1. **제빵 시장 골목 / new_bakery_market:** two bakery fronts, two narrow homes, two western delivery wagons, two eastern rubble clusters. The staggered western wagon lanes and breakable shops create a civilian market layout with alternative approaches.
2. **주둔병 포대 거리 / new_garrison_battery:** four cannon positions around narrow quarters, a warehouse and a fixed ruined building. The durable batteries retain their flanking detours after nearby buildings collapse.
3. **보급 창고 지구 / new_supply_warehouses:** four broad warehouses and four loading wagons, plus two rubble clusters. Wider building footprints create the longest measured radius-32 retreat detour in these definitions; warehouse destruction opens a shorter route while wagons remain.
4. **피난 수레와 붕괴 구역 / new_breach_refuge:** two fixed ruined shells, two intact eastern homes, two refuge wagons, one cannon and three passable rubble clusters. Durable western ruins and destructible eastern homes make different route choices.

The setting metadata is year 845. Every placed landmark references an existing raster asset through the exact existing prop catalog. No new bitmap, geometric substitute, rotated raster or invented asset ID was added. These are authored compositions, not empty chunks or random prop scatter; the recovered deterministic layout hash selects among the four compositions.

## Collision, destruction and rendering facts

The catalog alone defines collision/cover/destruction. Narrow/bakery/warehouse buildings are destructible with structure HP 3; collapsed instances use existing `burning_rubble`, an empty footprint and zero collision/cover masks. Ruined houses, cannons and wagons are durable. Burning rubble is passable and has zero contact damage; its name does not imply a fire damage hazard.

An intact house's ground footprint is only 44–60 pixels deep while its upright art can be 192 pixels tall. The renderer uses the catalog's full bounds and foot depth; these definitions do not replace those with collision rectangles. Slots keep solid footprints within each chunk and outside the cross streets. Test coverage establishes the declared geometric contracts, not hand-calibrated pixel contact.

Overlapping tall raster bounds, landmark visibility, player outlines, front/back occlusion, visible destruction cues and four-side contact must still be inspected through the integrated renderer in real play. Existing catalog calibration remains `candidate_pending_four_side_contact`. No visual/contact acceptance, finished-world claim, Android performance claim or gameplay quality approval follows from these tests.
